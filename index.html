Han Yu

I am an Associate Professor of Economics in the Fogelman College of Business & Economics at the University of Memphis. I am an applied microeconomist working on health economics, labor economics, and the economics of education. Another line of my research studies how culture shapes individuals' economic decisions and outcomes. I also serve as Director of the Methodist Le Bonheur Center for Healthcare Economics.

Before joining Memphis, I taught at Dalton State College and was a postdoctoral research associate at the Private Enterprise Research Center at Texas A&M University. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Louisiana State University in 2018. My research has been supported by the Social Security Administration and the FedEx Institute of Technology.
