Welcome

I am an Associate Professor of Economics in the Fogelman College of Business & Economics at the University of Memphis. I specialize in applied microeconomics, with a focus on health economics, labor economics, the economics of education, and the economics of culture. Another line of my research addresses subjective well-being. I also serve as Director of the Methodist Le Bonheur Center for Healthcare Economics.

My recent work examines the broad impact of public health policies, including Medicaid and medical marijuana laws, on crime, education, and the labor market. This research has been covered by The Wall Street Journal, The Economist, and National Public Radio, and cited in the New York City Bar Association's report to the Biden-Harris Administration and a U.S. Government Accountability Office report to Congress, among others.

Before joining Memphis, I taught at Dalton State College and was a postdoctoral research associate at the Private Enterprise Research Center at Texas A&M University. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Louisiana State University, and I also hold a master's degree in finance and a bachelor's degree in mathematics. My research has been supported by the Social Security Administration and the FedEx Institute of Technology.
